# OPIc 영어 연습 질문 200개

작성·온라인 자료 확인일: 2026-09-08

한국 OPIc 응시자를 위한 비공식 연습 문제집이다. 출제자의 관점에서 말하기 기능과 질문의 연결성을 고려해 구성했다. 공식 출제자가 작성한 자료나 실제 기출 200개를 수집한 자료는 아니다. 공개 모의고사·공식 설명 자료에서 주제와 유형을 조사하고, 아래 영어 문항은 독립적으로 새로 작성했다. 원문 질문을 그대로 옮기거나 일대일로 번역한 목록이 아니다.

## 구성과 사용법

- **Q001~Q160:** 자기소개·개인 배경·여가·여행·생활 주제 160개.
- **Q161~Q190:** 10개 상황 × 3문항 = 롤플레이 연계 30개. 각 세트는 정보 문의 → 문제 해결 → 관련 과거 경험 순서다.
- **Q191~Q200:** 비교·의견·변화·영향을 설명하는 심화 10개.
- 대괄호는 학습용 유형이다. 공식 난이도나 등급을 뜻하지 않는다. 주제 분류 역시 공식 설문 항목의 완전한 재현이 아니다.
- **한 Q번호가 한 문항**이다. 문항 안의 여러 질문은 답변을 구체화하는 보조 질문이다.
- 먼저 자신의 실제 배경에 맞는 주제를 연습한다. 직장·학교·해외여행처럼 경험이 없는 주제는 그 사실을 밝히고 관련 경험을 연결하는 연습에 활용할 수 있다.
- 답변은 영어로 녹음한다. 연습용으로 문항당 약 1~2분을 잡을 수 있으나, 이는 공식 제한 시간이 아니다.
- 모의 연습에서는 한국어 유형 표시를 가리고 영어 지문만 읽는다. 롤플레이에서는 행동 계획을 설명하는 대신 상대방에게 직접 말한다.

## 01. 자기소개·개인 배경 — Q001~Q010

- **Q001 [자기소개]** Introduce yourself to someone who has never met you. What would help that person understand your everyday life and interests?
- **Q002 [묘사]** Choose three words that describe your personality. Explain how one of those qualities appears in your daily life.
- **Q003 [일상]** Walk me through an ordinary weekday. Which part of the day do you usually look forward to most?
- **Q004 [인물 묘사]** Who do you spend the most time with these days? Describe that person and explain what you enjoy doing together.
- **Q005 [취향]** What activity helps you feel like yourself after a busy week? Explain what makes it satisfying for you.
- **Q006 [비교]** Compare a typical weekend now with a weekend when you were a teenager. What has changed the most?
- **Q007 [최근 경험]** Describe a small achievement you felt good about recently. What did you do, and how did you celebrate it?
- **Q008 [과거 경험]** Tell me about a time you met someone who later became a close friend. How did the conversation begin?
- **Q009 [변화]** Describe a habit you have changed in the past few years. What led you to change it, and how has your routine improved?
- **Q010 [계획]** What personal activity would you like to make more time for next month? Explain how you would fit it into your schedule.

## 02. 집·가구·집안일 — Q011~Q020

- **Q011 [묘사]** Imagine showing a visitor around your home. Describe the layout and the part of the home that feels most comfortable.
- **Q012 [사물 묘사]** Choose an item of furniture that you use every day. What does it look like, and how does it fit into your life?
- **Q013 [일상]** What happens at home during a typical evening? Describe what you do from the time you get home until bedtime.
- **Q014 [과정]** Explain how you clean and organize your living space. Which task takes the most effort, and why?
- **Q015 [비교]** Think of a place you lived in before. How does your current home differ in space, convenience, and atmosphere?
- **Q016 [과거 경험]** Tell me about a day when you rearranged or decorated your home. What did you change, and how did it turn out?
- **Q017 [문제 경험]** Describe a household item that stopped working at an inconvenient time. What were you trying to do, and how did you manage?
- **Q018 [과거 경험]** Recall a visit from a friend or relative at your home. How did you prepare, and what did you do together?
- **Q019 [변화]** Has the way you use your home changed since you were younger? Explain which activities now take up more space or time.
- **Q020 [선호]** If you could improve one part of your home, what would you choose? Explain the practical difference it would make.

## 03. 동네·이웃·지역 시설 — Q021~Q030

- **Q021 [묘사]** Describe the area within a short walk of your home. What would a visitor notice first?
- **Q022 [장소 묘사]** Which neighborhood facility makes your life easier? Explain where it is, what it offers, and how you use it.
- **Q023 [일상]** When you have a free hour in your neighborhood, where do you go? Describe your usual route and stops.
- **Q024 [인물·관계]** How do people in your neighborhood interact with one another? Describe a situation in which neighbors usually meet.
- **Q025 [비교]** Compare your neighborhood during the daytime and late in the evening. How do the sounds, crowds, and activities differ?
- **Q026 [최근 경험]** Tell me about a new place you recently discovered near home. How did you find it, and what was your first impression?
- **Q027 [과거 경험]** Describe a time someone in your neighborhood helped you or you helped them. What happened afterward?
- **Q028 [문제 경험]** Have construction, noise, or a closed road ever disrupted your neighborhood routine? Describe one incident and your response.
- **Q029 [변화]** What is one noticeable change in your neighborhood since you moved there? Explain how it affects your daily activities.
- **Q030 [의견]** What would make your neighborhood more pleasant for residents? Describe one improvement and who would benefit from it.

## 04. 영화·TV·영상 — Q031~Q040

## 07. 운동·조깅·자전거 — Q061~Q070

## 08. 해변·캠핑·야외활동 — Q071~Q080

## 14. 교통·약속·생활 서비스 — Q131~Q140

- **Q131 [일상]** Describe a journey you make regularly. Explain the route, the time it takes, and the part that requires the most attention.

## 17. 롤플레이 A — 예약·구매 — Q161~Q169

아래 각 세트에서 앞의 두 문항은 상황극이며, 세 번째는 상황극을 마친 뒤 자신의 경험을 설명하는 질문이다. 영어 지문의 문의 개수와 대안 개수는 이 연습 문제의 요구사항이다.

### 세트 A1. 독서 모임 공간 예약

- **Q161 [정보 문의]** You are organizing a small book club meeting at a café. Call the café and ask three or four questions to find out whether the space will work for your group.
- **Q162 [문제 해결]** The café has just told you that its meeting room is unavailable at your reserved time. Call the manager, explain what your group needs, and suggest two workable alternatives.
- **Q163 [관련 경험]** Leave the role-play now. Tell me about a time a group meeting location changed unexpectedly. How did everyone receive the new information, and how did the meeting go?

### 세트 A2. 자전거 대여

- **Q164 [정보 문의]** You want to rent bicycles for yourself and a friend for an afternoon. Speak to the rental shop employee and ask three or four questions before making the booking.
- **Q165 [문제 해결]** At pickup, only one of the two bicycles you reserved is available. Explain the situation to the employee and propose two ways to adjust your booking or your plans.
- **Q166 [관련 경험]** Leave the role-play now. Describe a time equipment or a service you expected was unavailable. What did you arrange instead, and were you satisfied with the result?

### 세트 A3. 요리 수업 신청

- **Q167 [정보 문의]** You are interested in a weekend cooking class for beginners. Call the organizer and ask three or four questions that will help you decide whether to register.
- **Q168 [문제 해결]** After registering, you learn that the class has moved to a day when you cannot attend. Call the organizer, explain the conflict, and offer two possible solutions.
- **Q169 [관련 경험]** Leave the role-play now. Tell me about a time the schedule of a class or activity changed after you signed up. How did you handle the change?

## 19. 롤플레이 C — 친구·생활 문제 — Q179~Q190

## 20. 심화 — 비교·변화·의견·영향 — Q191~Q200

아래 문항은 개인 경험에서 일반적인 설명으로 답변을 확장하는 연습이다. 특정 등급을 보장하거나 공식적으로 AL에만 배정된 문항이라는 뜻은 아니다. 지문 속 가정은 토론을 위한 설정이다.

- **Q191 [변화·영향]** Think about how people arrange social plans now compared with several years ago. What has become easier, and what new difficulties have appeared? Use an example you have observed.
- **Q192 [비교·의견]** Compare a neighborhood filled with small independent stores and one centered around a large shopping complex. How might everyday life differ for residents?
- **Q193 [의견·근거]** Some people choose leisure activities mainly because they look interesting online. How useful is that approach? Discuss both a benefit and a possible disappointment.
- **Q194 [문제·대안]** Imagine a popular local park becomes too crowded on weekends. What difficulties could this create, and what practical steps could improve visitors' experiences?
- **Q195 [변화·영향]** How might ordering meals more often change the way people cook and spend time together at home? Connect your explanation to something you have noticed.
- **Q196 [비교·의견]** Compare using a self-service machine with speaking to an employee when buying a ticket. Who might prefer each option, and what makes a service easy to use?
- **Q197 [의견·사례]** When planning a group vacation, how much should be decided together in advance? Explain how a group could balance shared activities and individual preferences.
- **Q198 [변화·영향]** Think of a hobby that people can now enjoy both online and in person. How does the online version change participation and the sense of community?
- **Q199 [문제·해결]** People sometimes find it difficult to keep work or study messages from interrupting their free time. Why can this be challenging, and what practical boundaries could help?
- **Q200 [의견·비교]** A community can improve either its outdoor recreation spaces or its indoor cultural facilities this year. Which would you prioritize for a place you know, and what trade-off would you accept?
